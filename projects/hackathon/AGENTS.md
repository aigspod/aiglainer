# hackathon

The aigs hackathon, from the aigs pod journal club's community: opening up machine-learned climate emulators
(SamudrACE, a coupled ocean-atmosphere emulator from Ai2 and E3SM, and ACE) with exege, the team's toolkit for latent
diagnostics, and ideas from four journal-club papers (Seeing Through Circuits, LAWFUL, whether circuits explain model
errors, transformers as dynamical systems). Source: the team's hackathon plan (exege `scratch/hackathon/PLAN.md`,
sections 0, 1, 3 and 4.2; read-only). Layout and commit rules: [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Facts the pieces may use, and nothing else

- The event is "the aigs hackathon". The date is one constant, `WHEN` in `videos/invite/event.py` (now
  "Week of November 16"); everything else (venue, times, prizes, registration) is "details soon". Don't invent any.
- Six tracks: ocean shortcut, ocean routing, atmosphere CO₂, errors and strata, dynamics, exege integration.
- Beginners and climate scientists new to interpretability are welcome.
